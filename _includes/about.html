About me

Hi! My name is Marin and I am currently a Post-doctoral researcher within the BioMechanical departement at TU Delft. I am working together with Professor Mathias Peirlinck on the numerical modelling of a soft total artificial heart for the Holland Hybrid Heart consortium .

Prior to this I completed my PhD at the Center for Doctoral training in Next Generation Computational Modelling (CTD-NGCM) at the University of Southamptonunder the supervision of Professor Gabriel Weymouth and Professor Georges Limbert

My academic background includes a Bachelor of Engineering (First Class) in Yacht and Powercraft Design, during which my dissertation received the (2017 RINA – BAE Systems Student Naval Architect Award - Final Year Project). Following this, I pursued a Master of Science (First Class) in Computational Fluid Dynamics, where I focused on the numerical modeling of ship maneuvering using computational fluid dynamics.
